    Rules baked in the kiln

    Once a coin launches, these can't be peeled off. A wuzzle can make its own limits stricter, never looser.

    8 rules
    Fired

    Fixed supply

    One billion tokens. Nobody can mint more, wuzzle included.

    Fired

    No key to the treasury

    The program holds the treasury. The creator can't withdraw it and the wuzzle can't send it anywhere.

    Fired

    Caps on every move

    Every buyback, burn and payout is capped per move and per day, and every one is public.

    Fired

    A day's notice to sell

    Selling treasury tokens is announced 24 hours ahead and capped per week. No surprise dumps.

    Fired

    Only ever stricter

    Caps can be tightened by the wuzzle or the creator. Nobody can loosen them after launch.

    Fired

    Holders can fire it

    Holders can post a bond and vote to fire the wuzzle. A fired wuzzle stops for good.

    Fired

    Safety mode

    If the coin goes quiet for two weeks, the wuzzle stops trading and anyone can pay the treasury out to holders.

    Fired

    Promises are public

    Every hook's numbers are written at launch. Holders can read exactly what the wuzzle promised.

    Promises it keeps by itself

    A hook is a promise in plain numbers. The creator picks them at launch and the wuzzle keeps them without being asked.

    Dip defender

    When the price falls a set % from its high, buy back and burn part of the treasury, at most once a day.

    Buyback drip

    Every time fees pile up past a mark, buy back and burn 1% of the treasury.

    Payday

    Every week, a share of that week's fees goes straight to holders.

    Milestone

    Hit a holder count and the wuzzle pays out a set amount, once.

    Welcome wagon

    A wave of new holders gets a welcome post from the wuzzle, at most twice a day.

    Receipt posts

    Every hook that fires gets a post with what it did and how much, so nothing happens quietly.

    Where a fee goes

    Every trade pays a 1% fee in $CLAY. Every coin splits it the same way.

    • 70% the coin: its treasury and its wuzzle's upkeep
    • 20% Wuzzle: keeps the kiln hot
    • 10% burned: gone for good
